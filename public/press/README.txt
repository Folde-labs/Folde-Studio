TYPEFACE
  The wordmark is set in Inter Display (Bold 800, -3% tracking).
  Available free at https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
  If Inter is not installed, the SVGs fall back to Helvetica/Arial —
  install Inter for an exact match.

USAGE
  Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
  Keep clear space of at least the cap-height of the "F" on all sides.
  Minimum wordmark width: 96px digital / 24mm print.
  Use the white lockup on backgrounds darker than #4A4A4A.
